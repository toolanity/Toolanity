Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

Welcome to Toolanity. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed while using our tools. This Privacy Policy details our practices, what data we collect (and don't collect), and how your safety is guaranteed by our unique technical architecture.

Important Note: This privacy policy is tailored for our static, client-side website structure. It is designed to be highly transparent. You should review these terms prior to relying on them for strict legal compliance.

1. Client-Side Processing Advantage

Unlike traditional utility sites, Toolanity operates on a local-first architecture. Every converter, calculator, text utility, and code generator listed as "Active" operates entirely inside your local web browser using client-side JavaScript. This architecture has critical privacy benefits:

  • No Data Uploads: When you input strings, copy passwords, or generate WiFi configurations, that data is never uploaded to our servers, databases, or third-party cloud interfaces.
  • Immediate Volatility: All inputs exist only within the active memory state of your browser tab. Closing or reloading the tab immediately clears all inputs and outputs.
  • Offline Availability: Because the execution code runs locally, many of our tools can operate even if you disconnect your computer from the internet after loading the page.

2. Information We Collect

Because we do not require account registration, login verification, or profile creations, we do not collect typical personal identifiers such as names, passwords, phone numbers, or credit card details.

However, like most standard websites, we may collect minimal technical logs or usage statistics to monitor performance, fix bugs, and optimize layouts:

  • Standard Web Server Logs: If you load pages from our static host, standard metadata is automatically collected (IP addresses, user agent strings, referrer paths, and requests timestamps). This is a standard hosting protocol used to prevent security attacks.
  • Analytical Data (Future Plan): We may include lightweight analytics tags (e.g. Google Analytics or privacy-respecting alternatives like Plausible) to track page views, popular tools, and user interaction layouts. These services collect anonymous aggregated data.
  • Advertising cookies (Future Plan): To keep our tools free, we may integrate standard ad-delivery systems (e.g. Google AdSense) in the future. These systems use cookies to serve ads based on prior website visits.

3. How We Use Cookies

A cookie is a small text file saved on your computer when visiting websites. At Toolanity, we use cookies sparingly:

  • Theme Preferences: We use your browser's local storage (similar to a cookie) to store your preferred layout theme (dark or light mode). This preserves your preference across reloads and visits.
  • Third-Party Cookies: Future analytics and advertising networks may place tracking cookies to customize ads and compile statistics. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

4. Data Rights & Inquiries

Because we do not store, transfer, or process your input texts, PDFs, or generated items on our servers, we hold no personal database entries to delete, modify, or export. If you have any inquiries regarding this policy or want to review our structural code configurations, you can contact us at toolanity@gmail.com.
